About the Art of Living Foundation

Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar founded the Art of Living in 1981 with a simple promise: a stress-free mind and a violence-free society. 45 years later it operates in over 180 countries through one of the world's largest volunteer networks, teaching breathing, meditation and yoga.

About the International Association for Human Values

IAHV was founded in Geneva in 1997 as the humanitarian sister organisation of the Art of Living, and holds special consultative status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council. In South Africa, IAHV programmes have reached more than 112,000 people across 327 projects, with 902 active volunteers. Its flagship is the Youth Leadership Training Programme.
